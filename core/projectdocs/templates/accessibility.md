## Checklist

_To fill: tick only what was actually tried, add what is particular to this UI, and write "Skipped: why" for what doesn't apply._

- [ ] Every action works with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus
- [ ] A screen reader announces controls, states and errors
- [ ] Text contrast at least 4.5:1, 3:1 for large text and controls
- [ ] Images have alternative text, decoration is hidden
- [ ] Forms have labels, and errors say what to fix
- [ ] Usable at 200% zoom and at phone width
- [ ] Nothing relies on color alone
- [ ] Motion can be reduced, nothing flashes
- [ ] Dialogs hold and return focus
- [ ] The page's language is set; video has captions
